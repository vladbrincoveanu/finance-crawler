Investment data platform

Identity-first ingestion for VIC ideas, Dataroma ownership, and HedgeFollow fund history. Curated facts stay clean; retrieval comes later.

Identity model

Investor

Reporting fund

One reviewed canonical fund or institution. Portfolio manager is separate metadata.

Company

Chapter parent

One reviewed company-level subject aggregating ideas, ownership, and events.

Security

Tradable instrument

Ticker, exchange, share class, or other identifier. A Company may own multiple Securities.

Trust boundary

Curated is the only public truth.
Source duplicates, unresolved identities, malformed rows, and quarantined content cannot feed APIs or retrieval.
Partial means partial.
Resolved rows may promote, but incomplete coverage is surfaced. Missing quarters never become invented sells.

Entity resolution flow

Phase 1 pages

  • /holdings/dataroma with one live Berkshire proof.
  • /holdings/hedgefollow with one live Berkshire crawl.
  • /holdings aggregate view.
  • Protected identity and quarantine review UI.
  • Source links show fetch and last-verified status.

Phase 1 proof

  1. Run current Dataroma baseline.
  2. Migrate empty database through versioned schema.
  3. Run one Berkshire source slice.
  4. Validate staging, identity, promotion, API, DOM, and links.
  5. Verify VIC legacy bridge does not truncate or duplicate source facts.
  6. Inspect Grafana dashboards and Uptime Kuma alerts.

Local observability

Metrics

Prometheus

Accepted, rejected, duplicate, latency, zero-row, and database health metrics.

Logs

Loki

Structured operational logs with source, run, parser, and correlation IDs.

Traces

Tempo

Direct SDK spans across crawler, validation, promotion, API, and database.

Dashboards

Grafana

Seven-day telemetry retention, quality panels, and configurable webhook alerts.

Checks

Uptime Kuma

Frontend, API, database, and crawler heartbeat checks.

Policy

Fail open

Telemetry outages never block data capture or curated promotion.
